    The desk

    A browser

    • Every new tab opens on your desk, with favorite sites in the dock
    • Tabs down the side, ad blocking, fast
    • Nothing new to learn for the 80% of the day you just browse

    A whiteboard

    • One board per task, spanning as many sites as it needs
    • The yellow note on top says what you're trying to get done
    • Pinch out from a page to its board, pinch in on a card to browse

    A memory

    • Copies and downloads land in the Cubby, tagged to the board
    • Closing a tab turns it into a card, with its catches clipped on
    • The day scrubber shows any board as it was
    The one new rule: closing a tab files it.

    Tabs opened from a board belong to it, and so do links you follow from them. Close one and it becomes a card on that board. Boards fill themselves, so nobody has to organize anything, and closing tabs is always safe.
