    New tab, no board needed

    Search and New tab always sit at the top of the sidebar. A new tab lands under Today, outside any board.

    Boards open in place

    Boards are listed in the sidebar. Open one and its easel and link bundles appear under it.

    The easel, one hover away

    Hover the easel tile and it slides out, joined to the sidebar. Write, move away, and it tucks back. Pin it for split view.

    Bundles, tied with string

    Links come in compact bundles. String ties each bundle to the easel idea it supports.

    Files live on the board

    Downloads made inside a board land on its shelf above the dock. The On this Mac button brings in files from your folders.

    Loose tabs join later

    Drag any tab from Today onto a board's name and Drift files it into the right bundle.
